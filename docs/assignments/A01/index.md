Task B: Product Analysis

I chose to analyze Fiskars scissors. The primary function of this product is to use convert an applied hand force into a shearing force between the two blades so that material is cut. 

The governing model for these scissors is torque. Torque = Force x distance (T=Fd)

This model shows that the scissors operate like a lever system. The hand applies force far from the pivot, thanks to the handles while the blades apply force to material at a shorter distance from pivot. One reasonable assumption is the scissors can be that the model is two rigid metals rotating about a fixed pivot. This allows us to ignore deformation in our calculations since chances are so small.
