# A6 – Bracket Drawing

## Objective 
This assignment builds off of last week's bracket design assignment by taking the design and designing it into SolidWorks and making a multi-view engineering drawing. I will need to create a parametric model to accurately design my bracket and dimension out my engineering drawing with appropriate tolerances when needed. 

## Parametric Design
I went back and listed every dimension I needed to design this bracket, and I accounted for stress and stiffness. To do this, I selected the highest allowed dimension between my calculations for stress and stiffness for every measurement, and this ensures that my design is in the allowed parameters.

## Drawing
The drawing was made by importing the bracket design and positioning the images to satisfy the third angle projection that was wanted. I then dimensioned everything making sure it was not messy or hard to read. Then I added tolerances to dimensions that needed to be exact in order for the design to work effectively. Finally, I added the necessary information in the lower right hand of the drawing.
