#About Me

Hello, my name is Maddux Bush, and I am an engineering student at UNC Charlotte, currently in my sophomore year. Though I am early in my engineering years I bring determination, passion, and an open mind into every problem I encounter. I enjoy taking a complicated problem, breaking it down, and finding the best solution that is both effective and efficient. 

My interest in mechanical engineering comes from wanting to understand the ins and outs of systems and machines that surround us every day. I have always been eager to learn about the newest technology or innovation breakthrough, and being a mechanical engineer means that I can be on the front lines of those breakthroughs one day. With the interest in everything technology and with my skills in my former years of school being more compatible with the STEM area, mechanical engineering was the obvious path forward for me. 

As I continue to be an engineering student at UNC Charlotte, I want to become someone who works effectively and efficiently when faced with challenges. I am working towards becoming an engineer who cares about the details, communicates with their team, and who is willing to learn from their mistakes. I realize that engineering is just as social as it is technical, so I have also been working to better my collaboration skills too, so I can become the best engineer that I can be.
